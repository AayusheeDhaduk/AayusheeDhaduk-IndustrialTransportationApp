## 2. Five Mandatory Questions & Answers

### 1. What is your business?
IndusMove is a specialized B2B **Industrial Transportation & Heavy Haulage Service** providing engineered multi-axle road transportation for oversized industrial machinery, power turbines, precast assemblies, and critical infrastructure equipment with dedicated civil pilot escort convoys and 24/7 central control room oversight.

### 2. What problem are you solving?
High-tonnage and over-dimensional machinery transport involves immense financial and logistical risk. Traditional freight booking lacks real-time GPS telemetry, axle-load safety monitoring, route corridor clearances, and instant route feasibility estimates. IndusMove provides complete cargo visibility, automated transit calculations, and milestone clearance verification.

### 3. Who is your customer?
- Industrial manufacturing plants (automotive, steel, cement, heavy chemicals).
- Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) infrastructure contractors.
- Energy, power generation, and wind turbine original equipment manufacturers (OEMs).
- Port authorities and coastal industrial staging terminals.

### 4. What does your app do?
- **Real-Time Satellite VTS Tracking:** Enter commercial Waybill ID (e.g. `IND-1025`, `IND-1026`, `IND-1027`, `IND-1028`) to view real-time GPS waypoint, speed, driver contact, and continuous neon energy transit progress.
- **Interactive Industrial Route Estimator:** Calculates estimated highway transit time, load trailer configuration, and efficiency percentage based on cargo profile and distance.
- **Operations Manifest & CSV Export:** Searchable fleet operations table and full manifest modal popup with live status filtering and real `.csv` download.
- **Commercial Freight Enquiry:** Validated multi-field heavy transport quote intake with specific validation requirements.

### 5. What information does your dashboard provide to the manager?
The Manager's Operations Dashboard provides immediate executive situational awareness:
1. **Total Managed Movements:** 1,248 Active Heavy Haul Consignments.
2. **Delivered Shipments:** 982 (78.7%) with gate receipt and e-Waybill clearance.
3. **In-Transit Movements:** 186 (14.9%) active on highway corridors.
4. **Regulated Convoys:** 80 (6.4%) operating under highway civil escort protocols.
5. **On-Time SLA Delivery Rate:** 99.4% on-time performance rate.
6. **Active Fleet Capacity:** 42 Multiaxle Hydraulic Trailers online and monitored.
7. **Interactive Filterable Charts:** Visual CSS status breakdown bars and an SVG on-time delivery metric ring.
