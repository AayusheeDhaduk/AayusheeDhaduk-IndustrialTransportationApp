STANDARDIZED OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

Engineered 4-Stage Movement Protocol

  1. 01 Cargo Survey & Rigging

    Weight verification, center of gravity analysis, and custom axle configuration.

  2. 02 Corridor Clearance

    Highway transit permits, civil pilot vehicle allocation, and toll clearances.

  3. 03 Telemetry VTS Transit

    Continuous satellite geofence tracking, speed compliance, and milestone reports.

  4. 04 Site Receiving & Handover

    Delivery staging, heavy crane de-rigging, and digital e-Waybill handover.
